# Spatial localization on unseen RAW-FABRID rolls

This follow-up was specified after the original RAW-FABRID, AITEX and grouped analyses. It asks whether the crop-denominator ranking reversal persists when the detector has seen no frames from the roll under test. All seven folds and both detectors will be reported regardless of sign. This is a within-archive robustness test.

**Folds and fitting.** Use the seven saved seed-13017 leave-one-roll-out folds. For each fold, use its original ordered 1,000 normal tile indices from the other rolls for both models. Verify against original archive metadata that no held-out roll or defective parent enters fitting. Freeze the published ImageNet weights. No target-roll normal samples are used to select channels, fit a bank or covariance, normalize scores, choose parameters or set thresholds.

**Models.** Keep the existing frozen ResNet-18 spatial PatchCore variant, including its 256-vector normal coreset and seed 13017. The independent comparison is a Swin-T transformer with a PaDiM-style position-dependent Gaussian score. Use torchvision ImageNet-1K V1 weights, the same grayscale-to-RGB normalization and 128 × 128 tile input. Average the 32 × 32 first-stage features down to 16 × 16 and concatenate the second-stage features on their native 16 × 16 grid. Select 32 of the 288 channels once with NumPy default_rng(13017), without replacement, in draw order. At each of 256 positions fit the normal mean and sample covariance with a fixed 0.01 identity ridge. Score by Mahalanobis distance divided by the square root of 32. These are fixed experimental settings, not the original PaDiM reference configuration.

**Paired policies and populations.** Test the 28 released and 28 fixed native windows on the same frames. Use identical source masks at intensity ≥128, nearest-cell expansion, maximum overlap fusion and the original 0.001 score bins. Primary pixel evaluation uses the exact 202 defective and 53 clean frames of the original primary analysis. Primary frame AUROC uses its original 303 frames. Also evaluate each model on all 709 held-out originals, including all 507 clean frames, as a prevalence sensitivity analysis.

**Decision.** Compute observed-area and whole-frame AP within each test roll. The fixed layout covers the full frame. Give each roll one vote in the primary mean of paired guided-minus-fixed AP differences. A reversal requires a strictly positive observed-area mean and strictly negative whole-frame mean, separately for each model. Also report both signs on individual rolls, all individual effects, the mean after deleting each single roll, and frame AUROC as a secondary endpoint. Compare the original spatial scores on the same primary frames using the same equal-roll statistic. Each fold fits its own reference distribution; this design avoids pooling uncalibrated scores across folds. The folds share training material and their deletion ranges are descriptive. No AITEX retuning or factory-level claim is part of this follow-up.
